🌸 Early 2000s Web Aesthetic 🌸

Early 2000s websites had a very unique look. Some iconic features included:

  • Scrolling sparkly GIFs
  • Pixel art borders
  • Glitter text made with “Glitterfy” or “Blingee”
  • Hit counters (“You are visitor #0001242”)
  • Guestbooks for visitors to leave cute messages
  • 3D WordArt titles from Microsoft Office

People built sites using tools like Geocities, Angelfire, and early HTML tutorials. Custom pages were often bright pink, full of sparkles, and full of personality!
