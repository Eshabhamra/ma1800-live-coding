✨ Early 2000s Social Media ✨

The early 2000s were the birth of modern social media as we know it today. Before Instagram or TikTok, early teens and young adults hung out on:

  • MySpace (2003) — known for glitter graphics, customized profile songs, and “Top 8” friendship drama.
  • LiveJournal (1999-2000s) — where people shared deep thoughts, emo poetry, and early fan culture.
  • Neopets (1999+) — technically not social media, but a huge community with forums, guilds, and avatar culture.
  • Habbo Hotel (2000) — pixel chat rooms where your avatar could sit on virtual couches and cause chaos.

These platforms helped shape online identity, personal aesthetics, and the rise of customizable profiles. The pink glitter-filled layouts and sparkly GIFs define early 2000s digital style.
