💖 Early 2000s Pop Culture Online 💖

The early 2000s internet was the birthplace of many iconic trends and communities:

  • Flash Games — Websites like Miniclip and AddictingGames ruled school computer labs.
  • Emo & Scene Culture — Fueled by MySpace photoshoots, heavy eyeliner, and bands like MCR.
  • Viral Videos — “Badger Badger,” “Numa Numa,” and “Charlie the Unicorn.”
  • Fanfiction — Hosted on Fanfiction.net, Quizilla, and LiveJournal.
  • Fashion Forums — Places like GaiaOnline and 2000s makeover doll websites.

The early 2000s were chaotic, sparkly, playful, and extremely creative — the foundation of meme culture today.
