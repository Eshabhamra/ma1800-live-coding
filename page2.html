💗 Early 2000s Technology 💗

The early 2000s were full of colorful gadgets and tech that felt futuristic at the time:

  • Flip Phones — The Motorola Razr (2004) became a fashion statement, especially in pink.
  • iPod (2001) — revolutionized music with its “1,000 songs in your pocket” slogan.
  • Dial-Up Internet — the *iconic loud connection noise* that took 30 seconds to load.
  • Windows XP (2001) — bright blue taskbars and bubbly buttons.
  • CD Burners — everyone made “mix CDs” decorated with Sharpies and glitter stickers.

Technology in the early 2000s was colorful, chunky, and often sparkly — matching the Y2K fashion and aesthetic of the era.
